About Our Program

The NPC Scholarship Program is dedicated to supporting talented students who demonstrate academic excellence and financial need. Our mission is to make quality education accessible to all deserving candidates.

Since our inception, we have helped thousands of students pursue their dreams and achieve their academic goals. We believe in investing in the future leaders of our society.

Scholarship Programs

🎓

Merit Scholarship

For students with outstanding academic achievements and test scores.

  • Up to $15,000/year
  • GPA requirement: 3.5+
  • Renewable for 4 years
💼

Need-Based Scholarship

Supporting students with demonstrated financial need.

  • Up to $12,000/year
  • Income verification required
  • Flexible renewal process
🌟

Excellence Award

Recognizing outstanding contributions to community service.

  • Up to $20,000/year
  • Leadership experience required
  • Mentorship included
